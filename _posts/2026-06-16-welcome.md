Hey, welcome! 👋

I'm Abdulloh Kafa Bihi, but you can call me Fabi. This is my official nickname, since my parents call me this. It actually comes from my last two names: ka**FA** **BI**hi. But well, some of my teachers/lecturers call me Kafa, Bihi, and the best of all: Kafi, haha (an absolute mispronunciation, pls don't call me this). I'm from Indonesia.[^names]

By day, I'm a biologist researching cancer metabolism and immunometabolism. This little site is finally my own corner of the web, after years of being scattered all over the place.

The serious stuff (research, publications, CV) lives in the pages up top. This blog is where I get to be a bit more myself. Things you can expect me to ramble about:

- Mountains, trail running, hiking, and endurance stuff in general. If it involves suffering uphill, I'm probably into it. I freakin love uphill, hell yeah!
- Some stuff and reviews about exercise physiology. Please note this is not my area of expertise, but I follow the research closely and then cheerfully use myself as the test subject. It's always fun to run an experiment and watch my time or [VO2 max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VO2_max) improve. It's kinda my hobby, and well, it's closely related to metabolism, so I'm not a total stranger to it. (I love high carb fueling 😎)
- Anime and manga. I'm a self-proclaimed Japanese pop-culture enthusiast. I've read and watched over 1000 titles. Well, maybe that's not something to brag about.
- I also write a lot about philosophy on my Indonesian blog. I'll try to write some new philosophy here too, in English. My main interests are [analytic philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_philosophy) and [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science). I like [Feyerabend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Feyerabend)'s *[Against Method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Against_Method)* and [Lakatos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imre_Lakatos)' *[research programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_program)*. Back when I was an undergrad, I read [Bertrand Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_Russell) and [Wittgenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_Wittgenstein); nowadays I don't have the time, haha. But I've shifted my focus onto [philosophy of biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_biology). I enjoy the back-and-forth discussion about the [somatic mutation theory (SMT)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/bies.201100022) of cancer and the [TOFT (tissue organization field theory) ](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/bies.201100025)of Anna M. Soto and Carlos Sonnenschein. Besides the analytic-adjacent stuff, I'm also quite interested in the more traditionally continental tradition, like [Camus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Camus)' [Absurdism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absurdism) (I've written an [8000+ word essay](https://fabifuu.wordpress.com/2019/06/28/girls-last-tour-dan-albert-camus/) about this one in Indonesian language), [Existentialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Existentialism), and whatever [Heidegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Heidegger) was smoking.
- Linux. I've been daily-driving it since 2019 and I have opinions. I use CachyOS btw. 😎
- And every now and then, my research, explained in plain language (if I have time).

In short, this is not a polished academic blog. More of a notebook for the stuff I care about. Have a look around, and if you want to say hi, my links are all in the sidebar.

See you around,
Fabi
